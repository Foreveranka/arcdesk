Fees

One fee, charged once, always visible in the quote before you sign anything.

Last updated 1 September 2026

The desk fee

2% of the maker's proceeds, paid by the taker. The taker is whoever accepts an existing order: the buyer when they hit an ask, the seller when they fill a bid. Posting an order to the book costs nothing beyond gas.

WhoPaysReceives
Maker (posts the order)Gas onlyTheir asking price in full
Taker (accepts the order)Price + 2% + gasThe USDC they bought

How a quote is built

For buying A Arc USDC from an ask priced at premium p:

proceeds = A × (1 + p)        → goes to the maker
fee      = proceeds × 2%      → goes to the desk treasury
you pay  = proceeds + fee

The order book shows every level as a single price per 1 Arc USDC with the fee already included, so what you compare is what you pay. Nothing is added at the last step.

Gas

You pay gas only on the chain where your own funds sit — one approval and one lock. The desk pays the gas for everything else, including the delivery on the other chain and returning expired liquidity to makers. On Arc the gas token is USDC itself; a full settlement costs the desk well under one cent.

What we never charge

  • No listing fee, no cancellation fee, no withdrawal fee.
  • No spread on top of the maker's price — the premium is the maker's, not ours.
  • No fee on refunds. If a trade fails you get back exactly what you escrowed.
