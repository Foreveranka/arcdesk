What Arcdesk is

Arcdesk is a software interface to escrow contracts deployed on Base and Arc. It is non-custodial: it never takes possession of your funds, never holds balances for you, and cannot move your assets. Makers set their own prices. The desk operates a keeper that submits routine transactions and charges a 2% fee to takers on completed trades.
